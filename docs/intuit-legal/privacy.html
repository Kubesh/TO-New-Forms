Privacy Policy

Treehouse Originals Operations (the "App") · Last updated: October 20, 2026

The App is a private internal operations tool built and maintained by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us") for Treehouse Originals ("Treehouse"). It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what information the App handles, in particular when it connects to Treehouse's QuickBooks Online company.

Who uses the App and whose data it is

Only authorized Treehouse staff, and the developer who maintains the App, can use it. The business data in the App and in QuickBooks belongs to Treehouse. We act on Treehouse's behalf and only to operate and maintain the App.

Information the App accesses in QuickBooks Online

After Treehouse authorizes the connection through Intuit, the App can:

  • read customer records (names, addresses, and the contact details held in QuickBooks);
  • read products and services (names, SKUs, IDs);
  • create invoices for Treehouse's customers, and read invoices it has created.

It uses the minimum needed for these purposes and does not read bank, payroll or tax information.

Information the App stores

  • The QuickBooks company ID and the IDs and names of the QuickBooks customers and items linked to the App's own customers and products;
  • for each invoice created: its QuickBooks ID, invoice number, total, and the related purchase order;
  • the connection tokens Intuit issues, which are stored encrypted;
  • Treehouse's own order, customer and product records, which the App holds independently of QuickBooks.

How the information is used

Only to run Treehouse's order and invoicing workflow. We do not sell the information, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it for any purpose other than operating the App for Treehouse. Information is shared with Intuit only as needed to make the QuickBooks connection work, and with the shipping service Treehouse connects separately for orders.

Security

The App runs on a private server that is reachable only over a private network by authorized users. QuickBooks connection tokens are encrypted before they are stored, and the key is kept separately from the database. Access to the server and database is limited to people who need it to run and maintain the App. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept while Treehouse uses the App. Treehouse can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from its Intuit account, which stops all access immediately, and can ask us to delete stored QuickBooks-related information, which we will do promptly. Information in QuickBooks itself remains in QuickBooks.

Cookies and tracking

This page does not use cookies or tracking. The App itself is an internal tool and does not use advertising or analytics trackers.
