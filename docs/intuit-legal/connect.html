Connecting or reconnecting QuickBooks

Treehouse Originals Operations

The connection to QuickBooks Online is made once by the app's administrator, who signs in to QuickBooks and approves access for Treehouse's company. Staff do not need to do anything.

If the app says the QuickBooks authorization has expired

QuickBooks authorizations lapse if they are revoked, or if the app goes unused for about 100 days. Tell the administrator ([CONTACT EMAIL]); they reconnect in a couple of minutes, and nothing is lost.
