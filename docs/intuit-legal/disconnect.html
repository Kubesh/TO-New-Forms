Disconnecting QuickBooks

Treehouse Originals Operations

You can disconnect the app from QuickBooks Online at any time, and access stops immediately:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as the company administrator.
  2. Open the Apps area and find "Treehouse Originals Operations" among the connected apps.
  3. Choose Disconnect.

After disconnecting, the app can no longer read or create anything in QuickBooks, and invoices already in QuickBooks stay as they are. To have the information the app stored about the connection deleted, email adrian@rznce.com; see the Privacy Policy.
